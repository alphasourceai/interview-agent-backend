Security and Data Protection Controls

1. Client and Candidate Data Isolation

  • alphaScreen is built as a multi-tenant platform.
  • Each client’s roles, candidates, interviews, reports, recordings, billing records, and team access are scoped to that client or authorized child entity.
  • Client users can only access data for the organization or entity scopes assigned to them.
  • Parent-level users may receive broader access only when explicitly configured.
  • Access controls are enforced server-side, not only in the user interface.

2. Role-Based Access Control

  • alphaScreen separates access by user role and scope.
  • Client-side permissions may include Super Admin, Manager, Member, and limited-access roles.
  • Sensitive actions such as managing users, billing access, entity administration, and candidate review are permission-gated.
  • This reduces the risk of internal client users seeing candidates, reports, or billing information outside their assigned scope.

3. Authenticated Dashboard Access

  • Client and admin dashboard users authenticate before accessing protected areas.
  • Dashboard and API requests are validated through authenticated sessions.
  • Protected service access verifies both identity and client/entity authorization before returning data.
  • Session validation is paired with server-side client and entity scope checks, so a valid login alone is not sufficient to retrieve another client’s records.
  • Admin access is separated from client dashboard access.

4. Encryption in Transit and Secure Transport

  • alphaScreen encrypts data in transit using HTTPS/TLS for browser, dashboard, candidate, API, file-delivery, and signed media access paths.
  • Authenticated dashboard requests and candidate workflow submissions are transmitted over encrypted transport channels.
  • Backend service integrations are designed to use encrypted provider connections and server-side credential handling rather than client-side credential exposure.
  • Transport encryption is paired with authentication and authorization checks so encrypted delivery does not replace access control.

5. Database, Auth, and RLS Controls

  • alphaScreen uses Supabase Auth for authenticated user sessions and Supabase-managed database and storage services for protected platform data.
  • Authenticated sessions are paired with database and backend authorization checks.
  • Database-side access policies are used as a guardrail, helping restrict records based on authenticated user and role context where applicable.
  • Backend service integrations remain server-side and apply explicit tenant, client, role, and entity checks before returning dashboard, billing, candidate, report, automation, or audit data.
  • Automation workflow records are protected through authenticated access patterns rather than public client-side data access.

6. Candidate Access Controls

  • Candidate-facing flows use controlled access paths rather than open dashboard access.
  • Candidate interview access is tied to invite and verification flows.
  • Accommodation and text-interview paths use controlled links with validation checks.
  • Candidate access is limited to the intended interview or submission workflow and does not expose dashboard data, other candidates, client records, or internal scoring configuration.

7. Controlled File and Recording Access

  • Sensitive artifacts such as resumes, reports, transcripts, and interview recordings are not treated as general public assets.
  • Recording and file access are controlled through backend authorization and private/signed access patterns where applicable.
  • Supabase Storage access controls are designed to work with Postgres RLS policies, allowing storage access rules to follow the same tenant and authorization model where applicable.
  • The application stores metadata separately from media files and controls access through authenticated access paths.
  • This helps prevent broad file exposure and limits access to authorized users.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • alphaSource generally retains client, candidate, hiring workflow, media, report, operational, billing, and account records only as needed to provide and support the service, maintain security and auditability, administer client accounts, comply with legal or contractual obligations, resolve disputes, and preserve business records where appropriate.
  • Candidate, screening interview media, transcripts, analysis, reports, and related hiring records are handled under alphaSource's retention and deletion procedures.
  • Retention periods may vary based on client agreement, service configuration, legal requirements, operational needs, security needs, backup lifecycle, dispute preservation, and account administration.
  • Billing, agreement, tax, payment, and account records may be retained where needed for legal, accounting, contractual, or business recordkeeping purposes.
  • Operational, audit, access, delivery, diagnostic, and security logs may be retained as needed for security, reliability, troubleshooting, auditability, abuse prevention, and compliance support.
  • Clients may request deletion or export of eligible records through support or the agreed administrative process.
  • Deletion and export requests are reviewed for authorization, scope, legal or contractual exceptions, security obligations, backup lifecycle, and operational feasibility.
  • Some information may be retained where required or appropriate for legal, contractual, financial, backup, security, abuse-prevention, or dispute-related reasons. Deleted data may persist for a period in backups or disaster recovery systems until normal backup lifecycle processes complete.

9. Data Minimization in Automation

  • alphaScreen automation is designed to support controlled review workflows while limiting unnecessary data exposure.
  • Automation may evaluate candidate records against configured criteria and prepare review items, approvals, or next-step workflow actions for authorized users.
  • Automation workflows are designed to avoid exposing unnecessary provider response data, provider credentials, transcript or media artifacts, or internal operational details to client users.
  • Candidate-facing next-step communications remain governed by configured approval and workflow controls.
  • Final hiring decisions remain with the client's authorized hiring team.

10. Auditability and Operational Logging

  • alphaScreen includes operational logging and audit-oriented records for important workflows.
  • The platform records request identifiers, workflow states, delivery or problem events for transactional email, and workflow status changes where appropriate.
  • Candidate automation is designed with reviewable workflow records so authorized teams can understand relevant actions and approval outcomes where appropriate.

11. Incident Response and Client Notification

  • alphaSource maintains incident response procedures for identifying, escalating, investigating, containing, remediating, and reviewing suspected security or privacy incidents.
  • Incidents are assessed based on severity, affected systems, data involved, client impact, legal or contractual obligations, and remediation needs.
  • Where alphaSource confirms an incident that materially affects client or candidate data, affected clients are notified as appropriate and in accordance with applicable legal, contractual, and operational requirements.
  • Notice timing and content may vary based on investigation status, scope, legal requirements, security considerations, and available facts.
  • Communications may include relevant information about affected systems or data categories, mitigation steps, recommended client actions, and support contact information where appropriate.
  • Details may be limited during an active investigation or where disclosure could increase security risk.

12. Email Delivery Monitoring

  • Transactional email is sent through a managed email provider.
  • Delivery/problem events are captured for monitoring.
  • This supports reliability for account setup, access, candidate communication, agreement workflows, and configured automation workflow communications.

13. Separation of Human Decision-Making and AI Output

  • alphaScreen is a decision-support platform, not an autonomous hiring authority.
  • Candidate reports, scores, summaries, and recommendations help hiring teams review candidates more efficiently.
  • Final hiring decisions remain with the employer.
  • Automation features are designed around human review and configured approval controls rather than automatic candidate advancement or rejection.

14. EEOC and ADA-Aligned Prompting and Accommodation Support

  • alphaScreen interview and review prompting is designed to focus on role-related qualifications, skills, experience, and job-relevant responses rather than protected characteristics.
  • Prompting and review language is structured to support consistent candidate evaluation while avoiding medical, disability-related, or protected-class inquiries unrelated to the role.
  • Candidate-facing workflows include accommodation-request paths and alternate text-interview support where appropriate, helping teams route candidate needs for human review.
  • The platform supports documentation of accommodation-related workflow status without exposing accommodation requests to unrelated users or dashboard areas.
  • EEOC and ADA alignment is supported through human review, accommodation routing, job-related prompts, and employer-controlled hiring decisions; alphaScreen does not replace the employer's legal obligations or individualized assessment.

15. Secure Development and Release Controls

  • alphaScreen development follows a QA-to-production workflow.
  • Changes are tested in QA before production promotion.
  • Known-good release tags are created before major changes so the system can be rolled back if needed.
  • Production-sensitive work is deployed in small, isolated patches with validation around authentication, permissions, candidate access, billing, scoring, and data exposure.

16. Vendor and Infrastructure Posture

  • alphaScreen uses managed infrastructure and service providers for hosting, authentication/database/storage, transactional email, AI analysis, and video interview capabilities.
  • Access to these services is controlled through secure provider configuration and backend-only service integrations.
  • Provider credentials, service keys, and internal integration details are not exposed to client users or candidates.
  • For video interview capabilities, alphaScreen uses Tavus as a managed video interview provider and limits integration access to backend-controlled workflows.
  • Tavus publicly describes its platform as enterprise-focused and states support for SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA, and BAA compliance; alphaScreen treats these as vendor posture statements and can review vendor documentation during enterprise procurement.
  • Video provider integrations are designed so client users and candidates do not receive provider credentials or unnecessary provider response details.

17. Confidentiality Commitment

  • alphaScreen treats client and candidate information as confidential.
  • Candidate information is not sold and is not used for unrelated marketing purposes.
  • Client and candidate data is used to provide the screening, reporting, interview, and hiring workflow services requested by the client.

Summary

alphaScreen protects data through encrypted transport, server-side tenant isolation, role-based access controls, authenticated access, managed database controls, controlled file access, private/signed media access patterns, retention and deletion procedures, operational audit logging, incident response readiness, email delivery monitoring, managed vendor integrations, EEOC/ADA-aware prompting and accommodation support, and approval-controlled automation design. The platform supports hiring teams while keeping final hiring decisions with authorized human reviewers.

This document is an overview of alphaScreen’s security-focused product architecture and operational controls. It is not legal advice and does not certify legal compliance for any particular employer or hiring process. Formal compliance certifications, contractual data processing terms, and client-specific security questionnaires can be addressed separately during procurement or contracting.
